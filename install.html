Install Naija Scramble.

Put it on your home screen like a real app — opens full-screen, loads instantly, and keeps working with no data. Takes about ten seconds. Pick your phone below.

Android · Chrome

Four taps on an Android phone.

01

Open the site

Visit naijascramble.github.io in Chrome. Any other Android browser works too, though the menu wording may differ slightly.

02

Open the menu

Tap the three dots ⋮ in the top-right corner of Chrome.

03

Install app

Tap “Install app” (or “Add to Home screen”), then confirm. Chrome sometimes offers this as a banner at the bottom — tap that instead if you see it.

04

Play

The Naija Scramble icon lands on your home screen. Open it any time — full-screen, no browser bar, works without data.

iPhone & iPad · Safari

Four taps on an iPhone.

01

Open in Safari

Visit naijascramble.github.io in Safari. This only works in Safari — Chrome and other browsers on iOS can’t install home-screen apps.

02

Tap Share

Tap the Share icon — the square with an arrow pointing up — in the bottom toolbar.

03

Add to Home Screen

Scroll down the share sheet, tap “Add to Home Screen”, then tap “Add” in the top-right corner.

04

Play

The icon lands on your home screen. Open it any time — full-screen, no Safari bar, works without data.
