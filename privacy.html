Privacy Policy.

Short version: we keep as little as we can, the prize-claim form is the one place we ask for a phone number, and that sheet is locked to the site owner only. Long version below.

What stays on your device

Your name on the leaderboard form, your mute setting, your best scores and unlocked badges, and your daily streak are all saved in your browser’s local storage. None of that leaves your device unless you submit one of the forms below — we don’t read it, and clearing your browser data clears it for good.

Suggesting a word

The Suggest a word form sends the word, category, hint, optional example sentence, and an optional name or handle (only if you choose to give credit) to a Google Form, which lands in a spreadsheet we review before adding anything to the game.

Posting to the leaderboard

The name you type after a Marathon run, along with your score, is posted to the public leaderboard — visible to anyone who opens the page. Use a nickname if you’d rather not use your real name.

Claiming the weekly prize — the one private form

The Claim prize form asks for your name, WhatsApp number, winning score, and network, so we can verify you’re that week’s leader and send the ₦1,500 airtime to the right number. This response sheet is kept private — owner-only access, never published, never linked to any page on the site. It exists purely so we can pay out and reach you if we have a question about a claim.

Anonymous traffic pings

Every page load quietly logs which page was visited, where you came from (or “direct” if there’s no referrer), whether you’re using the installed app or a browser tab, and your screen width. No names, emails, or IP addresses are collected by us in this ping — it’s just how we see which pages people actually use.

Who else touches this data

Every form on this site is processed by Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets, so Google’s own privacy policy covers that handling. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub’s privacy policy. We don’t run ad networks or third-party trackers on this site.

Cookies

We don’t set cookies. The only thing stored on your device is browser local storage, used solely for game state as described above.

Questions or a deletion request

Email naijascramble@hotmail.com and we’ll sort it — including removing a leaderboard entry or prize-claim record if you ask.
